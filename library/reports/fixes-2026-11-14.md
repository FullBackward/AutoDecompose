## Where I departed from the brief, with the evidence

1. **Item 2, subgoal children.** The name test assumes every child is a
   named declaration. 37 children are subgoals made by a tactic, named by a
   description (`case n % 3 = 0`, `forward`, `link 2: distribute the
   powers`); no identifier in the glue could name them. They carry
   `"subgoal": true` and are exempt from `glue-cites-children`, which is why
   the brief's script still prints 35 / 300. Their correctness rests on the
   review, as before; a later run could give each subgoal its own site
   inside the parent's range, which a tool could then check.
2. **Item 5, the FLT statement objects** `afa74779` and `b51da0fb` are not
   duplicates: their parents are different theorems
   (`IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.seven_pid` and `fermatLastTheoremFive`). They
   cluster because they share a context site and a generic goal shape. Both
   are kept and cross-listed.
3. **Item 5, the IMO 2026 Q4 trio** shares the statement and the top-level
   iff split, but the forward directions differ: Dirac uses one lemma
   (`exists_div_of_canGuarantee`), Axiom a counterexample
   (`equilateral_counterexample`), MechMath a rewrite by `arithmetic_bridge`
   and the invariants `safe_start` and `safe_nonwin`. Deprecating two would
   hide two decompositions of one problem, which is what AutoDecompose wants
   to compare, so all three are kept and cross-listed; this meets the brief's
   done-criterion. To follow the brief instead:
   `set_status.py <id> deprecated --reason "duplicate of lean-iff-split-mechmath-imo2026-75bedf39"`
   for `-axiom-imo2026-5ffbca5d` and `-dirac-imo2026-ff8df4eb`.
4. **Deprecated entries skip strict content checks.** The two shells have no
   claims and cannot meet them; "strict passes on all 91" holds because of
   this.
5. **The strict TODO check skips the README's Verification section**, which
   is rendered from review records: `lean-calc-chain-sphere-packing-7d6a60c4`
   (accepted) failed only because a reviewer's evidence mentions a TODO in
   the source.
6. **Glosses moved to the child's `note`**, not to the entry's `intent` or
   `consequences`, so each gloss stays with its child.
7. **`verified` requires `sorry-scan` recorded and not failed**, where the
   brief asks only that it be recorded: a hit inside a site is exclusion E3.
   The scan reads site ranges only, so a child site that stops before the end
   of its proof, or a lemma the children use, is outside it.
